A coupling between two probability distributions $\mu$ and $\nu$ is a joint distribution describing how mass from one distribution is matched to mass in the other.
$X \sim \mu$, and $Y \sim \nu$, then a coupling is any joint distribution of $(X,Y)$ who's marginals are correct
$$\Pi(\mu,\nu)=\{\gamma(x,y):\int \gamma(x,y)dy=\mu(x),\;\int \gamma(x,y)dx=\nu(y)\}$$
or intuitively (OT) $\gamma(x,y)$ tells how much mass at $x$ gets moved to $y$
