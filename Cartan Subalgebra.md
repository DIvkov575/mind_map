A maximal [[Subalgebra]] of a semisimple [[Lie Algebra]] that is abelian and acts diagonalizably. Intuitively: the largest set of generators you can *simultaneously diagonalize*. Its dimension is the **rank** of the algebra (e.g. rank $1$ for $\mathfrak{su}(2)$, rank $2$ for $\mathfrak{su}(3)$).

**What it organizes**

Diagonalizing the Cartan on a representation splits it into simultaneous eigenspaces; the eigenvalues are the [[Weight (Representation Theory)|weights]]. Doing this on the adjoint representation itself gives the [[Root System]] (the nonzero weights = roots), and each root supplies a pair of [[Ladder Operators]].

This is the backbone of highest-weight theory: Cartan $\to$ weights/roots $\to$ ladder operators $\to$ [[Irreducable Representation (irrep)|irreps]] and their [[Clebsch–Gordan Coefficients]].
