A bilinear form on a vector space $V$ is a map
$$
B:V\times V\to F
$$
that is linear in each argument separately:
$$
B(au+bv,w)=aB(u,w)+bB(v,w),
$$
$$
B(u,av+bw)=aB(u,v)+bB(u,w).
$$

A bilinear form may be symmetric, antisymmetric, degenerate, or indefinite. An [[Inner Product]] is the special case of a symmetric positive-definite real bilinear form.

See [[Multilinearity]].
