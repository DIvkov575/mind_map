measures how far two subsets of a metric space are from each other.

Let (M,d) be a metric space. For each pair of non-empty subsets, ${\textstyle X\subset M}$ and ${\textstyle Y\subset M}$ the hausdorff distance is defined as
$${\displaystyle d_{\mathrm {H} }(X,Y):=\max \left\{\,\sup _{x\in X}d(x,Y),\ \sup _{y\in Y}d(X,y)\,\right\},}$$![[Screenshot 2026-05-09 at 1.10.31 AM.png]]
