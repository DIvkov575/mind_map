In mathematics, for given real numbers  $a$ and $b$, $\log_b(a) = x$ such that $b^x =a$
⁡
The discrete logarithm generalizes this concept to a [[Cyclic Group]]. A simple example is the group of integers modulo a prime number (such as 5) under modular multiplication of nonzero elements.

More generally, in any [[Groups|Group]] G, powers $b^k$ can be defined for all k and the **discrete logarithm** $\log_b(a)$ is an integer k such that $b^k = a$
