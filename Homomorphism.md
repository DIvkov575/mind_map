Not to be confused with [[Homeomorphism]]

A homomorphism is a map between two algebraic structures of the same type (e.g. two groups, two fields, two vector spaces), that preserves the operations of the structures. This means a map ${\displaystyle f:A\to B}$ between two sets $A$ and $B$ equipped with the same structure such that, if ⋅![{\displaystyle \cdot }](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/media/math/render/svg/ba2c023bad1bd39ed49080f729cbf26bc448c9ba) is an operation of the structure (supposed here, for simplification, to be a binary operatino) then

${\displaystyle f(x\cdot y)=f(x)\cdot f(y)}$

for every pair x,y of elements of $A$ One says often that $f$ preserves the operation or is compatible with the operation.
