An endomorphism is a mathematical map, or [[Homomorphism]], from a mathematical object to itself.

More generally in [[category theory]], an endomorphism is a [[Morphisms]] from an object in some [[Category]] to itself.

An endomorphism that is also an [[Isomorphism]] is an [[Automorphism]].


For example, an endomorphism of a vector space V is a linear map f: V → V
and, an endomorphism of a group G is a group homomorphism f: G → G.
