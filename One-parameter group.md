one-parameter group or one-parameter subgroup usually means a [[Continuous]] [[Groups]] [[Homomorphism]] $\phi: \mathbb{R} \to G$ from the real line $\mathbb{R}$ (as an [[Additive group]]) to some other topological group $G$

Not actually a group, but homomorphism

Describe infinitesimal Transformations - infinitely small transformation of a parameter group that it generations
These transformations generate a [[Lie Algebra]] that is used to describe a [[Lie group]] of any dimension


The [[Group Actions|action]] of a one-parameter group on a set is known as a [[Flow]]. A smooth vector field on a manifold, at a point, induces a local flow (a one param group of local [[Diffeomorphisms]], sending points along integral curves).


**Definition**
A curve $\phi: \mathbb{R} \to G$ is called a one-param subgroup of $G$ if it satisfis the condition $$\phi(t)\phi(s) = \phi(s + t)$$
