
IVYWREL enrichment is a compositional proxy for thermostability. It measures the fraction of residues in:

I isoleucine, V valine, Y tyrosine, W tryptophan, R arginine, E glutamate, and L leucine.

  Higher fraction means the sequence looks more like proteins enriched in [[Thermophiles]] versus [[Mesophiles]].
It is not a direct melting-temperature predictor, and it is used here as
  an independent proxy
