mesh refers to the standard cortical surface (2-dimensional manifold that forms the outer layer of the [[Cerebral Cortex]]) mesh usewd by FreeSurfer for group-level neuroimageing analysis

`fsaverage` is a **template brain surface** created by averaging the cortical geometry of many subjects and representing it as a **triangular surface mesh**.

- Each hemisphere is represented as a mesh:
    - vertices (points on cortex)
    - triangular faces connecting them
