A map is smooth if it's infinitely differentiable ($C^\infty$): all partial derivatives, of every order, exist and are continuous. 

For [[Manifold|manifolds]]: a map $f:M\to N$ is smooth if its coordinate representation $\psi\circ f\circ\varphi^{-1}$ is smooth in the ordinary $\mathbb{R}^n$ sense, for every chart $\varphi$ on $M$ and $\psi$ on $N$.
