 random variable that prices all assets by weighting their future payoffs across states of the world. In the context of the Efficient Market Hypothesis, it provides the formal condition that links prices to expected payoffs under uncertainty
 $$P_t = \mathbb{E}_t[M_{t+1} X_{t+1}]$$
 The SDF $M_t$ is random because the value of money tomorrow depends on the state of the world
 - If a payoff occurs in **bad states** (when marginal utility is high), it is **discounted less** → asset is valuable.
 - If it occurs in **good states**, it is **discounted more**.
Thus the SDF embeds **risk preferences and intertemporal substitution**.
