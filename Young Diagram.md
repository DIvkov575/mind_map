<u>Def</u> A <u>Young diagram</u> $\lambda$ is a collection $| \lambda | = n$ boxes which are top and left aligned in a corner, one of [[Combinatorics]]'s standard combinatorial shapes.
	eg. 
	$\square\square\square$
	$\square\square$
	$\square$
	$|\lambda| = 6$

Filling a Young diagram's boxes with increasing integers gives a [[Standard Young Tableaux|Standard Young Tableau]].
