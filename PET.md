Polyethylene terephthalate (PET) is a thermoplastic polyester whose repeating chain contains [[Functional Groups|ester groups]]. It is used in fibers, bottles, thermoformed packaging, and oriented films.

PET can be amorphous and transparent or semi-crystalline, depending on processing and thermal history. It is strong, impact-resistant, chemically stable, and readily drawn into thin film. Biaxially oriented PET is sold under trade names including [[Mylar]].

For adhesive joints, smooth PET provides little mechanical keying. Bond quality therefore depends on cleanliness, wetting, adhesive chemistry, and sometimes roughening, corona/plasma treatment, flame treatment, or a primer. See [[Glue]] and [[Ethylene-Vinyl Acetate (EVA)]].
