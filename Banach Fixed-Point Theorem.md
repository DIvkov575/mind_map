In a [[Complete]] [[Metric Space]], any [[Contraction Mapping]]:
- has a unique point $x^*$ such that $f(x^*) = x^*$
- Iterating $f$ converges to $x^*$ from any starting point
	$x_{n+1} = f(x_n) \rightarrow x^*$
Contraction mappings are fundamental because they guarantee:
- Existence + uniqueness of solutions (e.g., differential equations, integral equations)
- Constructive convergence via iteration
- Stability in numerical methods and optimization
