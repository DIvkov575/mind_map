A **topological space** is a set equipped with a collection of “open sets”
Pair (X,T) where $X$ is a set and $T \subseteq P(X)$

Separation axioms grade how well $T$ can tell points apart — from $T_0$/$T_1$ up through [[Preregular space|preregular (R1)]] and [[Hausdorff Space|Hausdorff (T2)]] and beyond.
