An **isotopy** is a continuous deformation between two maps or embeddings over time

Formally, if f,g:X→Y are maps, they are isotopic if there exists a continuous family of maps
$$F_t : X \to Y \quad t \in [0,1]$$
such that
$F_0 = f$
$F_1 = g$

or
$F : X \times [0,1] \to Y$


Interpretation: you **continuously morph one object into another** without tearing or jumping.
Its like a paramatrized contiuous transform fo functions

In [[Knot Theory]], isotopy describes [[continuous|continuously]] deforming one [[Embeddings|embedding]]of a loop into another while keeping it embedded at every moment.
