 latent Dirichlet allocation (LDA) is a generative statistical model that explains how a collection of text documents can be described by a set of unobserved "topics." 

The core assumption of LDA is that documents are represented as a random mixture of latent topics, and each topic is characterized by a probability distribution over words. The model is a generalization of [[Probabilistic Latent Semantic Analysis (pLSA)]], differing primarily in that LDA treats the topic mixture as a [[Dirichlet Variable]] prior, leading to more reasonable mixtures and less susceptibility to overfitting. Learning the latent topics and their associated probabilities from a corpus is typically done using [[Bayesian Inference]], often with methods like [[Gibbs Sampling]] or [[Variational Bayes]]
