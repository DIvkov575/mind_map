A [[Groups|group]] where adidition is written as addition instead of multiplication;
Satisifies:
- Closure 
- Associativity
- Identity
- **Inverse:** For every $a \in G$ there is an element $-a$ such that $a+(-a) = (-a)+a = 0$

**Why "additive":**

In abstract algebra, a group operation can be written however we like. When the operation behaves like addition, mathematicians often write:
- identity as 000,
- inverse of aaa as −a-a−a,
- repeated operation as $na$

This contrasts with a **multiplicative group**, where the operation is written as multiplication, the identity is $1$ and inverses are written $a^{-1}$
