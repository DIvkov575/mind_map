**Geometry of generalized fluid flows https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.01434**
Recall that the hydrodynamical Euler equation for an incompressible fluid filling a closed compact [[Riemannian manifolds]] M is the following evolution law on the velocity field $u$
$$\partial_{t} u + \nabla_{u}u = -\nabla p$$
and supplemented by the divergence-free condition div u = 0 on M

Pressure function p is defined uniquely modulo an additive constant by those conditions. 
Note
$\nabla \tilde{p} = \nabla p +\nabla C=\nabla p + 0 = \nabla p$
$\partial_t u = \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}$
$\nabla \text{ is gradient}$
$\nabla_{u}{u}$ is taking directional derivative of vector field u in the direction of u
	$\nabla u$ directinoal derivatives of vec field
	$\nabla_v u$ directional derivatives in some idr
	$\nabla_u u$ directional derivatives in the direction we're already flowing
