The blocking or obscuring of something by another object.

- **vascular sense**: a blood vessel blockage (e.g. by a clot or plaque) — the mechanism behind [[pMCAO]] and behind arterial obstruction generally, see [[Ischemia]]
- **vision/ML sense**: one object in a scene blocking part of another from the camera's view — a standard failure mode for detection/segmentation/tracking models, which must infer the occluded region's extent from context rather than direct pixel evidence
