In this type of model, parallelism model weights, gradients, and optimizer states are split across devices. Tensor parallelism shards individual weights, which typically involves distributed computation of specific operations, modules, or layers of the model. Tensor parallelism is required in cases in which a single parameter consumes most of the GPU memory, or extremely large models such as GPT-3 that require partitioning over tens of instances.![[TensorParallelism.jpg]]
