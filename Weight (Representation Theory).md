A simultaneous eigenvalue of the [[Cartan Subalgebra]] acting on a representation. If $H_1,\dots,H_r$ are a Cartan basis, a weight vector $v$ satisfies
$$
H_i\,v=\lambda_i\,v,
$$
and the tuple $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_r)$ is its **weight**. A representation decomposes into weight spaces.

**Roles**

- The **highest weight** uniquely labels each [[Irreducable Representation (irrep)|irrep]] (highest-weight theorem) — the algebra's analogue of the spin label $j$.
- [[Ladder Operators]] move a vector between weights by $\pm$ a root ([[Root System]]).
- Rows/columns of the [[Wigner-D matrices]] are indexed by weights ($m$ quantum numbers).

Weights are the coordinate system in which [[Clebsch–Gordan Coefficients]] and equivariant feature "types" are expressed. See [[Representation Theory]].
