
Born Nicolas Luc-Olivier Merson in Paris, France, he grew up in an artistic household, the son of Charles-Olivier Merson, a painter and art critic. He studied under [[Gustave Chassevent]] at the [[École de Dessin]] and then Isidore Pils at the [[École des Beaux-Arts]]. Merson had his first work exhibited at the [[Paris Salon 1866]] and three years later was awarded the Prix de Rome. During the five years spent working in Italy, he concentrated on religious and historical subjects for his art.
