A central element of the [[Universal Enveloping Algebra]] — it commutes with every generator of the [[Lie Algebra]]. The quadratic Casimir is
$$
C_2=\sum_{a,b} g^{ab}\,X_a X_b,
$$
where $g^{ab}$ is the inverse of the Killing form (an invariant [[Bilinear Form]] on $\mathfrak g$).

**Key property**

Because it is central, Schur's lemma forces $C_2$ to act as a *single scalar* on each [[Irreducable Representation (irrep)|irrep]]. That eigenvalue therefore **labels the irrep** (for $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ it is $j(j+1)$). Casimirs are the algebra's intrinsic invariants — computed without ever choosing group coordinates.

**Two useful identities**

- On a [[Homogeneous Space]] $G/H$, $C_2$ realizes the Laplace–Beltrami operator — the link between rep theory and geometry/spectral methods.
- As an ML tool: use a Casimir of intermediate features as an invariant readout, or penalize features toward a fixed Casimir value to pin them to one irrep.
