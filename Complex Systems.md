study of systems with many interacting parts whose collective behavior cannot be deduced from the parts — no central controller, nonlinear interactions, adaptation. 

- lives between physics, biology, CS, and social science
- the defining puzzle: [[Emergence]]: macro-patterns (flocks, markets, consciousness) from micro-rules

**Categorization** — no canonical taxonomy; slices along three axes.

*1. mathematical core (the real content)*
- **[[Nonlinear Dynamics]] & [[Chaos]]**
	- feedback, bifurcations, sensitive dependence on initial conditions; attractors, Lyapunov exponents, edge of chaos. Synchronization: [[Kuramoto Model]], [[Oscillators]]
- **[[Network Science]]**
	- structure of interaction; scale-free, small-world, hubs, communities, percolation
- **[[Statistical Mechanics]] & [[Criticality]]**
	- phase transitions, [[Power Laws]], self-organized criticality, universality; [[Fokker–Planck]] as a workhorse
- Information & Computation: 
	- [[Information Theory]], [[Entropy]], Kolmogorov complexity, computation in nature
- **Evolution**
	- game theory, replicator dynamics, genetic algorithms, [[Evolutionary Search]], artificial life

*2. methodology*
- theory/analysis (dynamical systems, mean-field)
- [[Agent-Based Modeling]] — NetLogo → Mesa
- data-driven (empirical networks, scaling analysis)

*3. domain*
- systems biology · complexity economics · ecology/climate · urban scaling · infrastructure & swarms

**Objectives**
1. explain [[Emergence]]
2. find universal laws — power laws, allometric scaling $Y \propto M^{3/4}$, phase transitions recurring across domains
3. predict [[Tipping Points]] — regime shifts, critical slowing-down, before they happen
4. [[Robustness & Resilience]] — resilient to some shocks, fragile to others; where to intervene
5. understand [[Self-Organization]] — systems that structure and rewire themselves (ant colonies, immune systems, markets)
