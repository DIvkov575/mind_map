**p**ermanent **M**iddle **C**erebral **A**rtery **O**cclusion — a standard rodent model of ischemic stroke: the middle cerebral artery is permanently blocked (an [[Occlusion]]), inducing focal cerebral [[Ischemia]] in the downstream territory.

- "permanent" distinguishes it from **tMCAO** (transient MCAO), where the occlusion is removed after a set interval to also study reperfusion — see [[Ischemia]]'s note on [[Reperfusion injury]]
- used to study infarct volume, neurological deficit, and candidate neuroprotective/thrombolytic treatments in a controlled, reproducible lesion
