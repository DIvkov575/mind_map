A structure which is both 
a [[vector space]] (or more generally a [[Module]] over a [[Ring]])
and equiped with a multiplication operation that is compatible with the vector space structure

in other words; add, scale, and multiply elements


let $F$ be a field. An algebra over $F$ is a vector space $A$ over $F$ together with multiplication $$A \times A \to A, \quad (x,y) \mapsto xy$$ 

Algebra is said to *associative* given
$$(xy)z = x(yz)$$
Algebra is said to *unital* if there is an element $1_A$ st
$$1_A x = x1_A = x$$
