**[[Abstract Algebra]]**

In mathematics, especially in [[category theory]] and [[Homotopy Theory]], a groupoid (less often Brandt groupoid or virtual group) generalises the notion of group in several equivalent ways. A groupoid can be seen as a:
- [[Groups|group]] with a partial function replacing the binary operation;
- [[Category]] in which every [[Morphism]] is invertible. A category of this sort can be viewed as augmented with a unary operation on the morphisms, called inverse by analogy with group theory


**Algebraic Definition**
A groupoid can be viewed as an algebraic structure consisting of a set with a binary partial function. 
- non-empty set $G$ 
- [[Unary Operation]] $^{-1}: G \to G$ 
- [[Partial Function]] $*:G\times G\rightharpoonup G$ 
	- Not [[Binary Operation]] because it is not necessarily defined for all $g \in G$
Both operations have axiomatic properties
- Associativity
- Inverse
- Identity
