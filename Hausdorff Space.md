**T2 space** or **separated space**, is a [[Topological Space]] where distinct points have disjoint neighborhoods

Points x and y live in a topological space X can be separated by neighbourhoods if there exists a neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ and a neighbourhood $V$ of $y$ such $U$ and $V$ are disjoint


A topological space is Hausdorff if and only if it is both [[Preregular Space|Preregular]] (i.e. topologically distinguishable points are separated by neighbourhoods) and [[Kolmogorov Topological Space|Kolmogorov]] (i.e. distinct points are topologically distinguishable).
